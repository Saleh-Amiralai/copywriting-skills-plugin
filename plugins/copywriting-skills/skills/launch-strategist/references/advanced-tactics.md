# Advanced Launch Tactics

Optional amplifiers for a launch that already has the fundamentals (pre-launch, a solid conversion event, a real email sequence) covered and wants to push further. Not necessary for a first launch — the core frameworks in SKILL.md matter far more than any of these.

## Conversion optimization tools

- **On-page video Q&A** (e.g. VideoAsk) — lets a hesitant visitor ask a question right on the sales page instead of needing to email, and a personalized video reply back builds real connection. Even answering a handful of these during a launch often converts because people feel personally seen.
- **Exit-intent popups** — trigger when someone's about to leave the sales page, addressing one specific objection: "Wait — did you know we offer a 12-month payment plan for $299/month?"
- **Live chat/chatbot** — lowers the barrier for a quick question; can auto-handle the most common FAQs even when nobody's actively monitoring it.
- **Personalized outreach to repeat visitors** — if tracking shows someone's clicked the sales page 3-4 times without buying, a short personal Loom video ("noticed you've been checking this out — anything I can answer?") often converts fence-sitters better than another generic email.
- **Application funnels for high-ticket ($3,000+)** — replacing "buy now" with "apply now" pre-qualifies buyers, raises perceived exclusivity, and surfaces information that lets the pitch (and payment terms) be customized per applicant.

## Automation & engagement

- **Comment-to-DM automation** (e.g. ManyChat on Instagram) — a keyword comment triggers an automatic DM delivering a bonus, sales link, or answer, capturing attention and opening a conversation at scale.
- **SMS** — open rates run far higher than email (~98% vs. 20-30%), which makes it powerful for a small number of truly critical moments: final hours before close, a bonus about to expire, a webinar starting in 15 minutes. Get explicit opt-in first, and use it sparingly (2-3 texts across an entire launch) — overuse feels intrusive fast.
- **Messenger sequences** — light automated nurture after a webinar (replay delivery → engagement check-in → cart reminder as the deadline nears).
- **A temporary launch community** (Slack/Discord) — creates visible social proof (others engaging with the same content), lowers the barrier for questions versus email, and builds pre-purchase momentum through community energy.
- **A VIP channel** (Telegram or similar) for the most engaged leads — early bonus access, exclusive Q&A, a sense of being treated specially, which increases their likelihood of buying.

## Hyper-personalization (worth the effort mainly for high-ticket offers)

- **1:1 outreach to repeat clickers** — a short, genuine note offering to answer questions or hop on a 15-minute call.
- **Voice notes in DMs** instead of typed replies — feels more personal, and for a nuanced question is often faster than typing a long explanation.
- **Personalized Loom videos** addressing one person's specific stated objection or situation — takes a few minutes to make, but often converts because it demonstrates real understanding of their circumstances.
- **Segment-specific landing pages** — same core offer, but headline/examples/testimonials tailored per audience segment (e.g. coaches vs. course creators), routed via tags from a survey or past behavior.
- **"Chat with the founder" calls** — genuine fit conversations, not disguised pitches. Sometimes the right outcome is telling someone the offer isn't right for them — that honesty is itself what builds the trust that converts fence-sitters elsewhere.

## Traffic & visibility boosters

- **Podcast tour** in the 4-8 weeks before launch — value-first appearances on relevant shows that mention the upcoming training, reaching an audience that's already warm because they chose to listen to that show.
- **Guest expert collaborations** — cross-promotion with a complementary-niche expert (e.g. course creation ↔ Facebook ads), via list swaps or joint live sessions.
- **Affiliate/partner campaigns** — recruit partners with swipe copy, graphics, and unique tracked links; 30-50% commission is standard for digital products. A handful of affiliates with modest lists can meaningfully expand reach.
- **Retargeting ads** to webinar registrants and non-buyers — this is warm traffic (they already opted in once), so it reliably outconverts cold ad traffic; use it to remind them the replay/offer/deadline still stands.
- **LinkedIn outreach** for B2B offers — personalized, value-first connection requests tend to get better response rates than cold email because the platform is built for exactly that kind of networking.

## Scarcity & urgency mechanisms

The one non-negotiable rule underneath all of these: **never fabricate a deadline, a countdown, or a "spots left" number.** Getting caught faking urgency does lasting damage that no single launch's revenue is worth. Everything below only works — ethically and practically — when it's real.

- **Rolling/expiring bonuses** — different bonuses expire at different points through the cart (e.g. enroll in 24h → get bonuses A-D; by day 4 → B-D; by day 7 → C-D), creating several genuine mini-deadlines instead of concentrating all pressure on the final day.
- **Tiered pricing** — the price rises in real, permanent steps as the cart progresses (early-bird → regular → final-day price). The increase must actually happen and never get quietly walked back, or it teaches the audience that "final price" doesn't mean anything.
- **A limited "Founder's Circle" tier** — a small premium option with a real capacity constraint (e.g. can't realistically offer 1:1 calls to 200 people), which raises average order value while being genuinely scarce.
- **Better payment-plan terms for early joiners** — same total price, but more monthly-payment flexibility for people who commit early, rewarding fast action without penalizing anyone.
- **Cohort-based enrollment** — framing as "the March cohort" (capped for a real reason, like live coaching capacity) creates both scarcity and a sense of community that "customer #347 in an endless stream" never does.
