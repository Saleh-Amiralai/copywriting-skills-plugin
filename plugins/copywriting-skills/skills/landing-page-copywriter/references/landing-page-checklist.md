# Landing Page Conversion Checklist

Use this to audit an existing landing page, or as a final pass before shipping a new one. Not every item matters equally for every page — for an audit, identify the 2-3 highest-leverage fixes rather than treating this as a uniform pass/fail list.

## 1. Headline and Subheadline
- Headline immediately communicates the main benefit or solution — concise, engaging, relevant to the target reader.
- Subheadline complements the headline, adds clarity, and entices the visitor to keep reading.

## 2. Visual Design and Layout
- Strong visual hierarchy guides the eye naturally toward headline → CTA → supporting proof.
- Whitespace used strategically so the page is easy to scan, not cramped.
- Branding (colors, fonts, imagery) is consistent and cohesive.
- Images/video are high-quality and relevant — they enhance the message, not distract from the CTA.

## 3. Call-to-Action (CTA)
- The CTA button is visually dominant — contrasting color, clear placement, impossible to miss.
- CTA text is action-oriented and specific ("Get Started," "Download Free Guide," "Sign Up Now" — or better, benefit-driven).
- The CTA repeats at multiple points on longer pages (above the fold, mid-page, end) so it's always reachable without much scrolling.

## 4. Persuasive Copywriting
- Clearly addresses the target reader's main pain points or desires.
- Benefit-focused language — what the reader gains, stated concisely and compellingly.
- No unexplained jargon that could alienate a first-time visitor.
- Trust signals present (testimonials, case studies, reviews) to build credibility and pre-empt objections.

## 5. Social Proof
- Customer testimonials from real people who've benefited.
- Case studies with specific, relatable results for the target audience.
- Ratings, awards, or security badges included where relevant to reinforce trust.

## 6. Page Performance
- Fast loading time — test and optimize images, code, and scripts. Every extra second of load time costs conversions.
- Fully responsive on mobile — legible fonts, tappable buttons, elements that don't break on small screens.
- Verified to work consistently across browsers.

## 7. Form Optimization
- Form fields limited to only what's truly necessary — fewer fields generally means higher conversion.
- Clear labels, helpful error messages, and hints where needed.
- A brief privacy/data reassurance near the form to lower hesitation.

## 8. Navigation and Links
- Site navigation minimized or removed — nothing should give visitors an exit that isn't the conversion path.
- The path to conversion is straightforward, without unnecessary extra steps.
- No external links unless they're literally part of the conversion flow.

## 9. Trust and Transparency
- The unique value of the offer is clearly communicated — why it's worth taking action on.
- Privacy policy linked if the page collects personal information.
- Page uses a secure (HTTPS) connection.

## 10. SEO and Analytics
- Relevant keywords used in headline, copy, meta description, and image alt text.
- Meta description is engaging and includes the primary keyword, to entice clicks from search.
- Conversion tracking set up (bounce rate, time on page, conversion rate) so results are actually measurable.

## 11. Testing and Iteration
- A/B testing plan for major elements (headline, CTA color, imagery) to find what actually converts best for this audience.
- Heatmap or session-recording tools in place to spot where users click, scroll, or hesitate.
- A feedback loop — real user or customer input — feeding back into copy and functionality refinements.
